# cognition as the geometry of a vector field

what ibm-1 is ultimately for, and the standard its parts are judged against.
ARCHITECTURE.md says what exists; STATE.md says where we are; this says why any
of it matters.

the thesis in one line: **ibm-1 is not "a brain simulator with neurons." it is a
multi-timescale dynamical substrate in which a specific set of mechanisms jointly
determine the topology and plasticity of cognition.**

---

## 1. the formulation

take brain state at time $t$ as a vector over cortical, thalamic, hippocampal,
basal-ganglia, cerebellar, neuromodulatory, metabolic and structural fields:

$$
s=(x_c,\;x_t,\;x_{bg},\;x_h,\;x_{cb},\;n,\;\theta,\;e,\ldots),
\qquad
\dot s=F(s,u)
$$

cognition is **not** primarily symbols being passed around. it is the trajectory
generated by a nonlinear vector field

$$
\dot x=f(x,u;\theta,m)
$$

where $u$ is sensory and interoceptive forcing, $\theta$ is relatively slow
synaptic/structural state, and $m$ is fast modulatory state — thalamic mode,
neuromodulators, oscillatory phase, arousal, task context.

**the cognitive objects are geometric objects of $f$**: fixed points, limit
cycles, slow manifolds, metastable sets, heteroclinic channels, continuous
attractors, and their basins of attraction. a concept or working-memory item
need not correspond to one neural pattern; it can correspond to a region or
manifold $A_i\subset\mathcal S$ such that many microscopic states converge onto
approximately the same macroscopic trajectory. basin geometry supplies
categorical robustness: perturb $x$, and if it stays inside $B(A_i)$, the
dynamics reconstruct the cognitive state. cognition is then transitions
$A_i\to A_j$, with basin geometry determining robustness, ambiguity, completion,
competition and switching. [1]

**this is not a gradient system.** biological networks are generally
non-gradient, so $\dot x \neq -\nabla E(x)$. rotational flows, limit cycles,
travelling waves, asymmetric transition paths and metastability are all
available. "attractor landscape" here is shorthand for the topology of the vector
field and its invariant and metastable structures — never literally a hopfield
energy function. **this constrains implementations: an $f$ written as the
gradient of a scalar cannot express most of what we are after.**

## 2. the thalamo-cortico-thalamic loop is landscape control

TCT is special because thalamus modifies the **effective vector field** rather
than merely contributing another state variable:

$$
\dot x_c=f_c(x_c)+W_{tc}(m)\,x_t+W_{cc}(m)\,x_c,
\qquad
\dot x_t=f_t(x_t)+W_{ct}(m)\,x_c
$$

eliminating the fast thalamic variables locally induces an effective cortical
operator

$$
\dot x_c\approx f_{\mathrm{eff}}\!\left(x_c;\;W_{cc}+W_{tc}\,G\,W_{ct},\;m\right)
$$

so changing thalamic gain $G$, inhibition, synchrony or nucleus-specific routing
changes the **effective recurrent coupling of cortex**, and therefore the
jacobian

$$
J(x)=\frac{\partial f_{\mathrm{eff}}}{\partial x}
$$

— which modes are stable, which perturbations grow, where basin boundaries lie,
transition probabilities, and the dimensionality of the active manifold. **that
is a much stronger concept than "thalamus routes information."** cortico-
thalamo-cortical loops are anatomically interwoven with corticocortical
recurrence across sensory, motor and cognitive systems rather than forming a
separate relay system, and there is evidence for active thalamic control of
recurrent cortical dynamics. [2]

cognitive control is then **landscape control**:

$$
f(x;\theta,m_1)\;\longrightarrow\;f(x;\theta,m_2)
$$

same learned brain, different $m$, different effective attractor topology. one
task context deepens basin $A$, suppresses $B$, makes $A\to C$ accessible and
closes $A\to D$. attention, working memory, perceptual interpretation and much of
executive control become controlled changes in the stability and accessibility of
distributed neural trajectories, rather than discrete tokens moved through a
pipeline.

TCT sits near the middle of the bridge because thalamocortical recurrence
operates on **both** timescales: fast modulation of the currently accessible
dynamical subspace, and — combined with appropriately timed plasticity —
participation in changing the longer-lived geometry itself.

**but TCT is one operator inside a much larger coupled system. it is unusually
well-positioned to rapidly alter cortical effective dynamics. it is absolutely
not the whole story.** §5 is the rest of the story.

## 3. learning deforms $F$

learning acts on the slow variable. an experience traces
$\gamma: x_0\to x_1\to\cdots\to x_T$, and plasticity changes $\theta$ so later
dynamics are biased toward reconstructing relevant portions of it:

$$
\theta_{t+1}=\theta_t+\eta\,\Phi(\gamma,m)
$$

geometrically: create a new attractor, enlarge an existing basin, carve a
preferred transition channel between attractors, or bind previously separate
manifolds. **repeated experience does not store a literal sequence** — it
progressively changes $f$ until the sequence becomes an increasingly natural
trajectory of the system.

## 4. sleep performs offline trajectory-dependent edits

terminology first: this is **offline replay and systems consolidation**, not
reconsolidation. reconsolidation (mechanism 12) is the distinct phenomenon of a
previously consolidated memory becoming labile after retrieval and then being
restabilised.

during NREM the temporal organisation is nested:

$$
\text{cortical slow oscillation}\supset\text{thalamocortical spindle}\supset\text{hippocampal ripple/replay}
$$

the slow oscillation supplies coarse temporal windows; spindle-associated
thalamocortical dynamics create periods favourable for cortical communication and
plasticity; hippocampal ensembles replay compressed representations of recent
experience within those windows. [3][5]

so for a newly learned trajectory $\gamma_{\mathrm{step}}=(x_1,\ldots,x_n)$,
offline replay repeatedly samples a compressed approximation
$R(\gamma_{\mathrm{step}})$ under a special plasticity regime $m_{\mathrm{N3}}$:

$$
\Delta\theta_{\mathrm{cortex}}\;\propto\;\sum_k
\Phi\!\left(R_k(\gamma_{\mathrm{step}}),\;
\phi_{\mathrm{SO}},\;\phi_{\mathrm{spindle}},\;\phi_{\mathrm{ripple}}\right)
$$

**plasticity is phase dependent: identical neural activity at a different phase
has a different learning consequence.** this is not copying bytes
hippocampus→cortex. it is repeatedly perturbing the distributed cortical system
along correlated trajectories so that synaptic updates reshape its vector field.
afterward $\gamma_{\mathrm{step}}$ lies closer to an intrinsic high-probability
trajectory of cortical dynamics — deeper basin, stronger transition channel,
better error correction, reduced dependence on whatever originally indexed or
scaffolded it.

for motor behaviour specifically, avoid "hippocampus records footsteps and writes
them into cortex." motor learning involves motor cortex, striatum, cerebellum and
hippocampus depending on task structure, and different memory components
consolidate differently. the defensible abstraction:

$$
\text{online trajectory}\to\text{temporary distributed trace}\to
\text{state-gated offline reactivation}\to\Delta\theta\to
\text{changed future attractor/transition geometry}
$$

## 5. the twenty-eight mechanisms

each with the abstraction that matters for ibm-1, and its status against the
sealed registry. **audited, not aspirational.**

### acting on the instantaneous state $s$ and the field $F$

**1. local cortical recurrent dynamics — attractors/manifolds.** recurrent
excitation + inhibition produces metastable states, continuous attractors, limit
cycles, transient channels, travelling waves. a percept/concept/intention is a
metastable region $A_i$, not a neuron vector.
→ **declared**: `local_excitation`, `local_inhibition`, `microcircuit` topology,
`lateral_cortical_propagation`. **not realized** — see §6.

**2. laminar cortical computation.** cortex is not one recurrent sheet.
superficial/deep layers participate differently in feedforward, feedback,
corticothalamic and corticocortical interactions. pyramidal neurons perform
nonlinear coincidence computation — distal apical contextual input interacting
with basal feedforward input via dendritic spikes and bursting:

$$
y=f(x_{\mathrm{basal}},\,x_{\mathrm{apical}},\,m)
\quad\text{not}\quad y=f\!\left(\textstyle\sum_i w_ix_i\right)
$$

a cellular mechanism for context selectively changing which cortical trajectories
propagate. [1-dendrite]
→ **laminar structure declared**: `laminar_propagation`, `cortical_layers`,
`cortical_depth`, L1–L6 selectors.
→ **the two-compartment transfer function is ABSENT.** every implementation is
single-compartment. this is the highest-value missing piece in cortex itself.

**3. E/I circuitry.** PV/SST/VIP families alter gain, temporal precision,
competition and dendritic accessibility — continuously modifying the local
jacobian $J=\partial F/\partial x$ and therefore eigenmodes, stability and
effective dimensionality.
→ **declared**: `neural.{pv,sst,vip}.activity`, `local_inhibition`.

**4. cortico-thalamo-cortical recurrence.** §2.
→ **declared**: `thalamocortical_coupling`, `alpha_resonator`,
`corticothalamic_loop_lti`, `thalamic_nuclei` (LGN, MGN, VPM, VPL, pulvinar, MD,
TRN, sensory relay).

**5. basal-ganglia selection of state transitions.** conditional disinhibition —
BG need not contain the cognitive representation; it gates whether a cortical
policy/thought trajectory is permitted to unfold:

$$
P(A_i\to A_j\mid c,r)
$$

direct/indirect/hyperdirect pathways, dopamine-dependent learning. multiple
recurrent cortico-BG-thalamic subnetworks, not one global action selector. [3-bg]
→ **ABSENT.** `bg_territories` and `striosome_matrix` declared; no process
selects on either.

**6. dopaminergic reinforcement learning.** phasic dopamine as an
RPE-like signal acting on eligibility traces:

$$
\Delta w_{ij}\propto \delta_{\mathrm{DA}}(t)\,e_{ij}(t)
$$

credit can arrive *after* the trajectory that caused it, so reinforcement deforms
future basin-transition probabilities without symbolically representing the
rewarded sequence.
→ **declared**: `three_factor_stdp`, `neuromodulation` (VTA, SNc),
`eligibility_trace_transfer`. **but with no BG (5) there is nothing for the
selection signal to act on.**

**7. hippocampal fast episodic indexing.** DG/CA3 pattern separation + recurrent
pattern completion. an episode is an *address into* a distributed state
configuration, not a complete copy stored in one place.
→ **ABSENT.** `hippocampal_subfields` declared; no process selects on it.

**8. hippocampal sequence dynamics.** place-cell trajectories, theta sequences,
SWR replay — forward, reverse, reordered, prioritized, awake as well as asleep.
an internal mechanism for repeatedly evaluating trajectories without executing
them. [4]
→ **ABSENT.**

**13. working memory — multiple regimes.** persistent recurrent attractors,
dynamically evolving population codes, and *activity-silent* short-term synaptic
states. so WM need not mean $x(t)=\mathrm{const}$; information can remain
recoverable under a dynamical invariant while microscopic activity rotates
substantially through state space.
→ **ABSENT as a regime.** `neural.exc.nmda` is declared with the slow time
constant that would support persistent activity (`neural.py` says so
explicitly), but there is no short-term synaptic state variable, so the
activity-silent regime cannot be expressed at all.

**14. oscillatory coordination.** theta/alpha/beta/gamma are **not** named
cognitive variables. they are dynamical regimes altering excitability and
effective coupling; phase locking, phase resetting and phase–amplitude coupling
establish transient communication windows:

$$
W_{ij}^{\mathrm{eff}}(t)=W_{ij}\,g\!\left(\phi_i(t),\phi_j(t)\right)
$$

[7]
→ **declared, and native**: the temporal-spectral form makes phase a first-class
coordinate, so phase-dependent effective coupling is structural here rather than
bolted on. this is the single strongest fit between the substrate and the theory.

**15. neuromodulatory state.** dopamine, ACh, NE, 5-HT, histamine, orexin,
peptides act like **parameters of $F$**, not transmitted features:
$F(x)\to F(x;n)$ — gain, adaptation, S/N tradeoff, receptor kinetics, plasticity
thresholds, exploration/exploitation. same connectivity, qualitatively different
dynamics under wakefulness, threat, attention, novelty, sleep. [8]
→ **declared**: `neuromodulation` with `writes="parameters"` — the one place the
architecture already distinguishes writing state from writing the field.

**16. intrinsic neuronal dynamics.** adaptation currents, bursting, resonance,
bistability, plateau potentials, refractoriness, ion-channel state. even before
synaptic recurrence, $\dot x_i=f_i(x_i,I_i)$ contains memory and timescale
structure. neuronal state is not an instantaneous weighted sum.
→ **declared**: `neural.exc.adaptation`, `wilson_cowan_adaptive`, `ionic_*`.

**17. dendritic credit assignment.** dendritic branches as partially independent
nonlinear subunits; local spikes + bAPs + modulatory signals assign credit to
synapse subsets that jointly participated, rather than blindly modifying every
input. [9]
→ **ABSENT.** follows mechanism 2 — without compartments there is nothing to
assign credit to.

**18. cerebellar predictive control.** rapid supervised/error-driven adaptation,
temporal prediction, feedforward control, context→action mappings:
$(\text{state},\text{efference copy})\to\widehat{\text{future sensory state}}$.
note the caution that "internal model" may be too literal — some cerebellar
computations may be highly learned feedforward mappings that *behave* like
internal models. [10]
→ **ABSENT.** `cerebellar_lobules` and `cerebellar_microzones` declared; no
process selects on either.

**19. efference copy / corollary discharge.** actions inject predicted
consequences back into sensory dynamics, separating self-caused from
externally-caused sensory change. without it, perception during saccades,
locomotion and speech is catastrophically ambiguous. [11]
→ **ABSENT.** `efferent_propagation` exists; the return path does not.

**20. predictive hierarchical cortical dynamics.** independent of any strong
predictive-coding ideology: reciprocal hierarchical inference where higher-order
state constrains lower-order interpretation while mismatch perturbs higher-order
state; extended to active sensorimotor prediction rather than passive
perception. [12]
→ **ABSENT as a mechanism.** the *substrate* exists (`laminar_propagation` has
the feedforward/feedback laminar asymmetry, `tract_propagation` the long-range
reciprocity) but no process computes or propagates a mismatch.

**21. active sensing.** the brain does not infer a fixed world from passively
arriving measurements. eye/head movement, whisking, locomotion, touch and speech
actively change $u(t)$:

$$
\dot x=F(x,u,a),\qquad \dot e=G(e,a)
$$

cognition is fundamentally a closed brain–body–world dynamical system.
→ **ABSENT.** both halves of the loop exist as processes (`transduction`,
`afferent_propagation`; `efferent_propagation`, `effector_activation`) and the
loop between them is not closed. **this is the same missing closure as STATE.md
§7b** — the video→EEG→dynamics→EEG′→video loop is mechanism 21 with TRIBEv2
standing in for body and world.

**22. sensorimotor primitives and cortical manifolds.** training need not store a
sequence of motor commands; it can create low-dimensional dynamical trajectories
such that high-dimensional musculature is driven by stable population modes.
learning makes desired trajectories increasingly *intrinsic* to the dynamics.
→ **not declarable, and should not be.** this is a predicted *consequence* of
1 + 3 + 6 + 23 under training — an outcome to measure, not a process to write.
if it does not emerge, something upstream is wrong.

### acting on the slow parameters $\theta$ and the architecture $\mathcal G$

**9. systems consolidation.** §4. offline reactivation deforms cortical dynamics
until partial cues reconstruct the manifold without hippocampal scaffolding. [5]
→ **ABSENT** (requires 7 and 8).

**10. NREM temporal gating.** N3 is not "memory write mode." SO organizes
up/down states, spindles provide nested windows, ripples coordinate with both, so
$\Delta\theta=\Phi(x,\phi_{\rm SO},\phi_{\rm spindle},\phi_{\rm ripple})$. [5]
→ **ABSENT.** `plasticity` does not read oscillatory phase. **this is cheap and
high-value**: the spectral form already carries $\phi$ as a coordinate, so the
plumbing exists — the plasticity rule simply does not consume it.

**11. synaptic homeostasis / renormalization.** learning cannot monotonically
strengthen everything; synapses compete under local and global constraints. sleep
appears related to this recalibration, though strong universal-downscaling claims
remain debated. geometrically this prevents the landscape from saturating. [6]
→ **declared**: `homeostatic_scaling_lti`.

**12. reconsolidation.**
$A_{\rm old}\xrightarrow{\rm retrieval}A_{\rm labile}\xrightarrow{\rm new\ context}A'_{\rm stable}$
— how the system updates an old attractor rather than eternally appending
immutable episodes.
→ **ABSENT.**

**23. structural plasticity.** spine turnover, axonal remodeling, receptor
trafficking, myelination, intrinsic excitability change the architecture on which
faster plasticity operates. the timescale ladder:

$$
x:\;\text{ms–s}\qquad m:\;\text{s–hours}\qquad
\theta:\;\text{min–months}\qquad \mathcal G:\;\text{days–years}
$$

→ **declared**: `plasticity`, `structural.*`, `activity_dependent_myelination`.

**24. astrocytic / extracellular regulation.** second-order but not nonexistent:
transmitter clearance, extracellular ions, metabolism, NMDA co-agonism — modifying
local excitability and plasticity *fields* rather than carrying representations.
[13]
→ **declared**: `extracellular.*`, `structural.gliosis`, `transmitter_dynamics`,
`ionic_diffusion`.

**25. metabolic/vascular constraints.** computation is energy-constrained;
delivery, neurovascular coupling, temperature and ionic restoration constrain
which high-activity attractors can persist. **a resource term in the dynamics,
not measurement noise.**
→ **declared**: `metabolism`, `neurovascular_coupling`, `vascular_flow`,
`tissue_exchange`, `thermal_diffusion`.

**26. developmental priors.** anatomy is not randomly initialized. genetic
gradients, cell types, areal parcellation, projection biases, spontaneous
developmental activity and critical periods establish a highly structured
$F_{\theta_0}$. **lifetime learning searches locally around an already extremely
non-generic dynamical system.**
→ **ABSENT as dynamics, but this is what the whole prior system IS.** the
three-tier priors, measured group connectome, microcircuit priors and
`cortical_association` distance prior are exactly $F_{\theta_0}$. what is absent
is critical-period *dynamics* — priors that themselves change with maturation.

**27. offline generative regimes beyond N3.** awake rest, quiet pauses, NREM, REM
and dreaming permit internally generated trajectories under different
external-input and neuromodulatory regimes. **do not collapse "sleep" into one
optimizer** — different offline regimes likely optimize different things.
→ **ABSENT.** `eval_sleep_state` classifies stages; nothing changes the
generative regime as a function of stage.

**28. multi-timescale eligibility.** the abstraction tying much of this together —
events leave decaying hidden traces

$$
e_k(t)=\int_{-\infty}^{t}K_k(t-\tau)\,\psi_k(x(\tau))\,d\tau
$$

on which later dopamine, replay, spindle phase, dendritic spikes or novelty can
act. **this is what lets causally related information separated by milliseconds,
seconds, minutes or hours meet at the plasticity rule.**
→ **declared**: `three_factor_stdp`, `eligibility_trace_transfer`. and the
spectral form is unusually well suited to it — a decaying exponential kernel
$K_k$ is one pole, so a trace is a first-class transfer function rather than an
extra buffer.

### the compression

$$
\boxed{
\begin{aligned}
\text{fast neural dynamics} &\Rightarrow \text{trajectories}\\
\text{recurrent topology} &\Rightarrow \text{attractors/manifolds}\\
\text{thalamus + inhibition + oscillations} &\Rightarrow \text{dynamic accessibility}\\
\text{basal ganglia} &\Rightarrow \text{trajectory/policy selection}\\
\text{cerebellum} &\Rightarrow \text{fast prediction + correction}\\
\text{hippocampus} &\Rightarrow \text{rapid indexing + replay}\\
\text{neuromodulators} &\Rightarrow \text{global dynamical/plasticity regime}\\
\text{plasticity} &\Rightarrow \text{deformation of }F\\
\text{sleep/offline dynamics} &\Rightarrow \text{reorganization across timescales}\\
\text{body/environment loop} &\Rightarrow \text{closed-loop cognition}
\end{aligned}}
$$

and the bridge:

$$
\boxed{
\text{biophysics}\to f(x;\theta,m)\to\text{attractor/manifold geometry}
\to\text{cognitive dynamics}\to\text{experience-dependent deformation of }f
}
$$

## 6. the audit, and the two things that block everything

**tally: 12 of 28 declared and materialized. 1 (mechanism 22) correctly
undeclared as an emergent consequence. 15 absent.**

### 6.1 the whole forebrain outside cortex and thalamus is anatomy without dynamics

no process selects on any of these. `Anat(...)` appears in process declarations
for exactly four systems — `thalamic_nuclei`, `cortical_layers`, `cortical_areas`,
`brainstem_nuclei`. the rest:

| anatomy | status |
|---|---|
| `bg_territories` | requested by 3 library models; **no process** |
| `striosome_matrix` | requested by 2 library models; **no process** |
| `hippocampal_subfields` | requested by 4 library models; **no process** |
| `cerebellar_lobules` | requested by 3 library models; **no process** |
| `cerebellar_microzones` | referenced **nowhere** outside its own definition |
| `amygdalar_nuclei` | referenced **nowhere** outside its own definition |
| `hypothalamic_nuclei` | used by topology builders only; **no process** |

this is worse than "unused." **models in the library request readout from
striatum, hippocampus and cerebellum, and no process generates dynamics there** —
so whatever those models report for those regions comes from generic cortical
processes wearing a subcortical label. that is a correctness problem in the
library today, not only a gap in the mechanism list.

it maps exactly onto absent mechanisms **5, 7, 8, 9, 18**. the anatomy,
partitions and supports already exist; only process declarations and
implementations are missing. **cheapest real progress available.**

### 6.2 nothing cognitive can emerge until the dynamics are nonlinear and bounded

§1 says cognition is metastable sets, basins and transitions. the materialized
graph has **no cycle**, and `build` selects LTI for all 29 processes. a linear
system has exactly one fixed point: no metastable sets, no basins, no
transitions, **nothing for a cognitive object to be**.

the one edge that would close the cortical loop is `potential→rate`, whose only
$f$ is a sigmoid. 27 `Form.RATE` implementations are declared, so the
nonlinearity is available — but the one nonlinear run attempted diverged
(7.5e15 mV) at prior parameters.

**this gates every mechanism above and everything in §7.** ordering constraint in
STATE.md §7c: fit the RATE implementations, then check the nonlinear dynamics are
bounded and have more than one invariant set, and only then close a loop onto
them. closing a training loop onto an unfitted nonlinear system trains against
divergence.

## 7. why this can be bridged *en training*

these mechanisms do not all have to be hand-specified, and that is the point of
the closed-loop regime in STATE.md §7b. **the declared form CONSTRAINS and the
data FILLS IN:**

- a process declares $(I,O,T)$ and a family of $f$ with priors; training moves
  $\theta$ within that family. the topology is the inductive bias; the parameters
  are learned
- an unbounded naturalistic stimulus stream supplies the trajectories $\gamma$
  that plasticity needs, at a volume no paired-recording corpus can reach
- the measured-recording likelihood anchors the result so the learned $f$ stays a
  brain rather than becoming whatever fits the loop
- mechanism 26 is why this is tractable at all: we do not search the space of all
  vector fields. the priors put us near an already extremely non-generic $F$, and
  training searches locally

so the arc for each absent mechanism is: **declare it, give it a weak prior, let
the trajectories deform it** — not specify a hippocampus and hope.

the ordering that follows from §6:

1. **nonlinear and bounded** (§6.2) — everything else is void without it
2. **close the loop** (mechanism 21 / STATE.md §7b) — this is what supplies $\gamma$
3. **subcortical processes** (§6.1: mechanisms 5, 7, 8, 18) — anatomy is already there
4. **phase-gated plasticity** (mechanism 10) — $\phi$ is already a coordinate
5. **dendritic compartments** (mechanisms 2b, 17) — the deepest change to cortex itself
6. **curriculum → cognitive schema → RL** (STATE.md §7c) — mechanisms 6 and 9 doing
   what they are for

## 8. can we identify cognitive and affective structure in the dynamics?

against the six-dimensional proposal in *The Shape of Experience*, "The Geometry
of Affect" (theshapeofexperience.org/part-2/the-geometry-of-affect). the audit
below is what ibm-1 could actually compute, not what it aspires to.

### 8.1 the strongest point of contact is the asymmetric metric

that framework requires $d(A\to B)\neq d(B\to A)$ — fear slides into anger
easily and the reversal is costly; grief does not retrace backward — and
concludes it needs a Finsler/Bregman-like divergence rather than a symmetric
distance.

**a non-gradient flow gives this for free, and a gradient flow forbids it.** if
$\dot x=-\nabla E(x)$ then transition costs are governed by differences of a
single scalar and the barrier structure is far more constrained. asymmetric
transition costs between basins require rotational / non-gradient components in
$F$. so §1's refusal of a scalar energy is not a stylistic preference — it is the
structural precondition for the asymmetry that framework treats as the signature
of affect. **this is a shared prediction and a falsifiable one: if our fitted $F$
turns out to be near-gradient, the affect geometry cannot be there.**

### 8.2 the six dimensions, against what ibm-1 has

| dim | definition | status in ibm-1 |
|---|---|---|
| **arousal** $\mathrm{KL}(b_{t+1}\|b_t)$ | belief update rate | **unusually well-matched.** most substrates have no belief state; §4.1 built one. uncertainty propagates as spectral gaussians, so $b_t$ is a real object and the KL is closed-form |
| **effective rank** $(\operatorname{tr}C)^2/\operatorname{tr}(C^2)$ | active dimensionality | computable from any trajectory. **meaningless until §6.2** — on an LTI graph it reports the rank of the input through a transfer function, not a property of the brain |
| **integration** $\Phi$ | irreducibility over the min-information partition | **more tractable here than usual.** the partition search is otherwise superexponential; our topologies (`microcircuit`, `local`, `tractometric`, `cortical_association`) declare the coupling explicitly, so the bipartitions are enumerable over a known structure. use their proxy $\Delta P=L_{\rm pred}[\text{part}]-L_{\rm pred}[\text{full}]$ |
| **valence** | gradient descent on distance to the viability boundary $\partial V$ | **we have a principled $\partial V$ that most frameworks must stipulate.** mechanism 25 is a real resource term: `metabolism` outputs `metabolic.atp` and `metabolic.consumption`, `vascular_flow` and `neurovascular_coupling` bound delivery. non-viability can be *metabolic infeasibility* rather than a hand-drawn set |
| **counterfactual weight** | compute on non-actual / total | **structurally blocked.** needs a simulator subregion — mechanisms 8 (replay) and 18 (cerebellar forward model), both absent |
| **self-model salience** $I(z^{\rm self};a)/H(a)$ | self-model driving action | **structurally blocked.** actions exist (`effector.drive`, `effector.force`), so $a$ is real; the *return* does not — mechanisms 19 (efference copy) and 21 (active sensing), both absent. no $z^{\rm self}$ to take mutual information with |

three computable, one with an unusually good grounding, two blocked by
mechanisms already on the §6 list in the order already recorded.

### 8.3 the affective periphery exists; the affective centre does not

worth stating precisely, because a name-based search misses it. the machinery
that IS declared:

- **neuromodulator state**: `extracellular.{dopamine, serotonin, acetylcholine,
  noradrenaline, adenosine}`, read by `neuromodulation`, which is the one process
  that `writes="parameters"` — mechanism 15 done properly
- **interoceptive transduction**: `transduction.baroreceptor` on `viscera`, plus
  `nociceptor`, `thermoreceptor`, `chemoreceptor`, with `blood.pressure` and
  `blood.oxygenation` as inputs
- **the metabolic resource term** that could ground $\partial V$

what is absent is the centre: `amygdalar_nuclei` is referenced **nowhere** outside
its own definition, and `hypothalamic_nuclei` has no process (§6.1). so ibm-1 can
represent the autonomic periphery of an affective state and the global
neuromodulatory regime it induces, and has nothing in between.

### 8.4 the identification bridge is the real problem

**locating a metastable set is well-posed. calling it *grief* is not** — not from
dynamics alone. geometry gives you $A_i$, basins and transition asymmetries; it
does not give you labels. labels require paired data where a subject reported
something.

the corpus already has the right bridge, and does not hold it:

| source | what it supplies | binding |
|---|---|---|
| `deap` | 32 subjects × 40 videos, 32ch EEG **+ peripheral physiology**, per-trial valence/arousal/dominance/liking | **unbound**, licence unresolved |
| `mahnob-hci` | EEG + gaze + physiology + video, emotion labels | unbound |
| `cmu-mosei`, `iemocap` | affect labels, no neural | unbound |

DEAP is the closest thing to a direct test: it is simultaneously the neural
signal, the interoceptive channel we already declare, and the valence/arousal
plane. 60 of 601 cards touch affect; acquiring DEAP is the single highest-value
one.

### 8.5 the honest answer

**not today.** every one of the six is either trivial or undefined on a system
with one fixed point (§6.2): no basins to have asymmetric transitions between,
and an $r_{\rm eff}$ that reports the input.

**after nonlinearity and loop closure:** arousal, effective rank and integration
become genuinely measurable, valence becomes measurable with a non-arbitrary
$\partial V$, and the gradient-vs-non-gradient character of the fitted $F$
becomes a real falsifiable test of §8.1. counterfactual weight and self-model
salience wait on mechanisms 8, 18, 19, 21.

**what we would have is the geometry, and geometry is not yet phenomenology.**
that is the correct ceiling to claim, and it is still a lot: an affect framework
that makes structural predictions about $F$ can be *tested against a fitted $F$*,
which is not something the field can currently do.
