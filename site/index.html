The Implicit Brain Model (IBM-1) is a multimodal foundation model of whole-brain state and dynamics. One parameter set is declared once — as fields, anatomy, topologies and processes — and task-specific models are materialized out of it on demand. Because the representation is tied to the brain rather than to a measurement, film, MEG, EEG and motion capture all constrain the same parameters — data that could not otherwise be combined at all.

§1the design

M = materialize(R, r, B, F, A, T, P)

Four primitives, and one operation over them. An explicit model names regions R, a resolution r(q) and a bandwidth B(q); dependency tracing then instantiates only what is reachable from that target. The same field can sit at 1 mm across the brain and 50 µm around a single electrode. 44 models are declared this way and differ only in what they ask for.

fieldsF = { x(q) : q ∈ Ω }

state variables on a support. a physical quantity, never an encoding.

anatomya(q) ∈ [0,1]ᵏ

soft membership in named partitions. evidence about where things are.

topologiesT(i, j)

which state variables may interact, through what. all spatial structure lives here.

processesP = (I, O, T, f, θ)

dynamics over a topology. implementations compete for a process.

§2 Six things traced out of one declaration. Each names what it was measured against; the two marked declared are built but not yet run, and say so.

01 · materialized

Read the picture someone is looking at

measured

An image goes into a declared optic nerve, through cortical dynamics, and out as 64-channel EEG — then back, retrieving which of 200 images a person was shown.

  • 63.5%top-1 on the corpus's designated test set, 200 images, median rank 1. Chance is exactly 1/200, so this is 127× chance.
  • 48.4× → 1.00×through the sheet to a precentral-only readout with ports verified disjoint, against a severed sheet. The severed arm has across-image variance of exactly zero.
  • 8.69% → 0.44%when the optic nerve port is displaced to a random region of equal size. Where the structure is declared is doing the work.

The control that bites: a dynamics-free encoder, selected the same way, matches this. The substrate is load-bearing inside the model; it has not beaten a purpose-built baseline.

02 · materialized

A body that stays upright because a cortex is in the loop

measured

The same substrate drives a full musculoskeletal body — 1,047 bone and muscle surfaces, 117 tissue force elements — closing the loop between cortical dynamics and muscle.

  • 0.17 mmcentre-of-mass displacement over 250 exchanges and five seconds under a 5 N push to the pelvis.
  • on the floor at 2.90 swith the cortex severed — 499 mm of drift. The dynamics are necessary as a path.
  • 11.5°worst joint excursion past a declared range while crawling 0.920 m, against 145° for an earlier attempt withdrawn for folding the ankles back on themselves.

The control that bites: a kernel with its site rows permuted holds the body up just as well. This shows the cortex is the motor owner. It says nothing about whether what it learned contributes.

03 · materialized

One kernel, many instruments

measured

The thesis itself: EEG, MEG, BOLD and fNIRS forward models are not four codebases but four views of one parameter set, so every corpus constrains every task.

  • 88.7%of a task-specific kernel recovered by the fused implicit kernel, on the task that kernel was trained for. A random kernel recovers 0%.
  • 16 → 1materializations trained simultaneously to step 32,000 out of one 3.84M-parameter kernel. Replica drift cosine 0.998–1.000; none collapsed.
  • exactly 0out-of-band movement when real EEG is fused into the posterior, while in band it moves 2.03 prior sd. Delays as phase ramps agree to 4.4e-16.

Reported as failures, not omitted: 2 of those 16 — video and audio_visual — sit at negative skill against persistence.

04 · materialized

Speech, sleep, and the rhythms in between

measured one arm withdrawn

Declared oscillators, fitted to real overnight recordings, reproduce textbook rhythms from single physical constants rather than from a fitted spectrum.

  • 1.000 Hzfor the slow oscillation, ± 0.296 across 8 scored N3 nights, reproduced from one declared constant: τadapt = 0.12 s.
  • 13.45 Hzfor the thalamocortical resonance — a spindle, not the alpha the prior declared. Held out at +5503 nats/night, p = 7.2e-11.
  • 4.220×chance on speech→MEG retrieval over 2,820 held-out windows, against a linear ridge's 2.89×.

The control that bites: removing the cortex entirely scores 4.397×, and never training it scores 4.223× — all indistinguishable. Training 19,200,005 parameters moves the result by 0.003. That arm is an envelope tracker, and the regression head it replaced reads +0.0022 correlation against an SE of 0.0025: no signal at all.

06 · declared, not yet run

Predict how a spectrum reorganises over weeks

no result yet

The application that would make the rest worth something clinically: given a modulation protocol, predict not the pulse-by-pulse response but the long-term reorganisation of the power spectrum — which is what a course of treatment is actually chosen for. plasticity_learning is the declared materialization.

What exists today: ibm/processes/plasticity.py and the plasticity_learning materialization are declared over the hippocampal and neuromodulatory systems. Nothing has been run. There is no longitudinal corpus bound yet, and this is the furthest of the six from a number.

  • 0.00correlation between predicted and observed band-power change at 4 weeks — placeholder
  • 0.0×skill against a persistence baseline that predicts no change — placeholder
  • n = 0subjects, 0 sessions, corpus not yet bound — placeholder

The control it will need: persistence. Spectra drift on their own, so "predicts the change" must beat "predicts no change" by more than the test–retest spread — on both sides of the comparison, not one.

§3how it was trained

The spine above carries no chronology on purpose. Training order is a different fact, and only the training line is on it: film and audio as a primer, then paired MEG, then EEG, with motion capture next. Width is checkpoints released.

§4try it

Materialize a model from the declaration

Three light dependencies — numpy, scipy, pyyaml. No weights, no GPU, no corpus. This runs the thing the whole page is about: naming a target and watching dependency tracing decide what has to exist.

!pip install git+https://github.com/JacobFV/IBM-1

import ibm
ibm.load_all(seal=True, strict=True)   # the whole ontology, validated

m = ibm.materialize("eeg_forward")
print(m.describe())                    # regions, resolution, bandwidth,
                                       # and only what the trace instantiates

Run the released implicit kernel

One checkpoint carries the parameters for any materialization; the per-task checkpoints are a convenience for compute and memory, not a different model. Heavier — this tier wants jax and, for some targets, a GPU.

from huggingface_hub import hf_hub_download

ckpt = hf_hub_download("jacob-valdez/ibm-1",
                       "implicit/ibm1.implicit.s30k.e128.fused34.pt")

m = ibm.materialize("eeg_forward").load(ckpt)   # fused from 34 checkpoints
m.run(...)
