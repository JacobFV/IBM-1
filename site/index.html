a research substrate · updated 2026-09-05 · github.com/JacobFV/IBM-1

ibm-1 is a multiscale probabilistic dynamical substrate — fields, anatomy, topologies, processes — from which task-specific explicit brain models are lazily materialized. an EEG forward model, a hemodynamic model and a next-frame video predictor are views of one posterior, not three models.

it sits at the bridge between measured neurophysiology and the cognitive structure that emerges from attractor dynamics. the physiology end is declared, falsifiable, and has already lost six arguments to data. the cognitive end is not declared at all, because a schema or a percept is a property of the vector field, not a parameter you set.

§1the four primitives

resolution, bandwidth, datasets, observations, interventions and explicit models are all expressed through four primitives rather than introduced as additional ones.

fieldsF = { x(q) : q ∈ Ω }

an indexed family of state variables. a component is a kind of physical quantity — membrane potential, K⁺ concentration, flow rate — and never an encoding of something else. fields do not share a support: the cortical sheet, the vascular tree and a sensor array are different domains with different notions of distance.

anatomya(q) ∈ [0,1]ᵏ

partitioning systems that map a position into soft memberships over named partitions: 34 cortical areas, 31 thalamic nuclei, 36 brainstem nuclei, 96 skeletal muscles. an atlas is evidence about where things are, not a coordinate system.

topologiesT(i, j)

which materialized state variables may interact through some class of process. tracts, laminar circuits, the vascular tree, the lead field from cortex to a contact. spatial structure lives entirely here; there is no universal spatial operator.

processesP = (I, O, T, f, θ)

dynamics over state variables connected by one or more topologies. the pair (f, θ) is implementation — linear, conductance-based, learned — selected per regime and swappable without touching the declaration.

§2materialization

an explicit model is a lazy materialization of the implicit one:

M = materialize(R, r, B, F, A, T, P)

you name spatial regions R, a resolution r(q) over those regions, a bandwidth B(q), and which fields, anatomies, topologies and processes you need. dependency tracing then instantiates only the state variables and processes reachable from the target. the same field can exist at 1 mm across the whole brain and at 50 µm around an electrode at the same time, without instantiating 50 µm state everywhere. resolution earns its cost only where coarse-graining stops commuting with the dynamics.

because the process graph is finite, the core clique is shared across nearly every materialization; R, r and B are the only levers by which the forty entries in the library differ. every materialized model carries provenance: which f was selected per process, which parameters were moved off their prior by evidence and which were not, and where a process was run outside the regime in which its f is meaningful. a prediction resting on prior-dominated structure is not presented with the confidence of one resting on constrained structure.

the graph in the opener is this idea drawn literally. every cortical, subcortical and sensor position is the mne sample subject's own — an oct5 sampling of its pial surface coloured by its FreeSurfer parcellation, its aseg segmentation, its digitised EEG cap and the 102 magnetometer positions of its MEG array. click a materialization and what lights up is read off the request symbolically: the regions it names, the devices it declares, the state it targets. the retina, cochlea and spinal stub are drawn for orientation; this subject has no geometry for them, and neither does the model pretend to.

the implicit structure exists independently of what any instrument can observe or any materialization can afford. evidence constrains what it constrains; elsewhere the structure remains at its prior. that is the machinery working, not failing.

§4what the cortex is for

a video generator with a decorative brain still shows a falling loss. a real head's MEG prices any cortical state that does not produce that field.

the paired MEG stage is the anchor of the curriculum and the reason it comes early. in a self-supervised loop both encoder and decoder are learned, so nothing stops the model routing information around the dynamics and using them as a delay line. the first eval harness caught exactly this in its first use: targets had been standardized by an artifact-dominated per-run variance, so predicting zero scored 0.0065, and what had been reported as 91–97% of variance explained was in fact a skill of −12 to −4. every metric is now skill against explicit baselines, including trivial ones.

a kernel that cannot subtract can only blur

with all-positive, fan-in-normalized association weights, eight applications per forward are eight rounds of averaging: effective rank fell 1.57 → 1.01 while reconstruction diverged 2.04 → 6.66. a signed kernel holds rank at 3.40 and reconstruction falls 1.56 → 1.48. cortical association is not all-excitatory. on the audio-visual run the occipito-temporal edges came out at mean |w| 0.618 against 0.139 for random pairs — 4.4× the baseline, net excitatory, and genuinely signed.

the objective was once parasitic on its substrate: |v|max drifted 64 → 430 mV while the loss kept falling, because a 430 mV membrane predicts frames perfectly well. a one-sided viability penalty is what makes the objective mutualistic, and it is in the checkpoint name for that reason.

cognition is not primarily symbols being passed around. it is the trajectory generated by a nonlinear vector field.

§6the curriculum

the organizing identity is

∇ log p(θ | D) = ∇ log p(θ) + Σd ∇ log p(Dd | θ)

a curriculum is a schedule over d. materializations are more terms in the same product rather than competing regimes, and the factorization is the parallelization: the multi-materialization trainer fits an audio-visual head and a paired-MEG head onto one shared 32.0M-parameter substrate in a single optimizer step, so anything the two teach each other has to pass through the association kernel. the stages are a gated DAG, and a stage does not begin until its gate is measured green.

  1. fan-in-aware gain priorthe divergence was a declaration problem: |L(0)| = 7218 because the gain prior was per edge and a node has 4118 incoming edges
  2. regime selectionagainst sleep-EDFx N3; swept at τadapt = 0.30 s: zero divergence, oscillation and multistability both present
  3. per-source spectral fitjoint θ beats the literature prior on all four sources, and beats each source's own fit on none
  4. forced sensory alignmentforcing constrains delta and essentially nothing above it: 0.5–4 Hz r² +0.0033, 8–13 Hz −0.0001
  5. the closed video loopunbounded data, and the stage where per-site tying finally has something to spend 73M parameters on
  6. audio-visual materialization68.9M parameters, 20,000 steps, 4 hours on one GB10; recon 1.77 → 0.185
  7. the paired MEG anchorskill +0.957 at cortical rank 3.42 through a rank-64 lead field
  8. multi-materialization, then development, then schemacritical periods, cognitive schema and RL are declared as stages, not yet reached

sequencing matters for capacity too. the declared association kernel is resolution-independent by construction — 2,882 parameters whether there are 13.5k sites or 570k — and flipping to per-site tying at 0.5 mm buys 73M parameters whose posterior equals their prior. a larger model and not a better one, which is why it waits for the closed loop's unbounded data. throughput: caching edge weights per forward rather than rebuilding the similarity inside every dynamics step took a step from 2.76 s to 0.67 s at 250k sites, so one epoch over the 11-minute movie is 0.77 GB10-hours.

a claim about what forcing constrains that does not name a band is not a claim.

§7where it stands

the honest distance to the cognitive end is that there is no cycle yet.

the materialized graph currently selects a linear implementation for all 29 processes, and a linear system has exactly one fixed point. there is no attractor landscape, so there is nothing for a cognitive structure to be. the periphery — 6 anatomical systems, 192 nerve labels, 483 ports over 96 named muscles and 9 receptor surfaces — produced the first closed feedback cycle in the declared process graph, with segmental reflex bandwidth at 16.7–35.7 Hz against 1.8–3.3 Hz for the long cortical loop. the EEG forward materialization is still acyclic.

  • stage 0 convergence. |L(0)| < 1 is unblocked for the training path and still owed for eeg_forward.
  • re-run the ablation. long-range edges now take a flat patchy prior; the fix is not confirmed until the cortex ablation is repeated.
  • give the task cue content. the seam where the cognitive stage enters.

gaps stated plainly: no held-out split evaluation, no free-running rollout evaluation, no seed or variance reporting. every result on this page is one run. weights live in a private hugging face repository; no licence has been declared for the code yet.
